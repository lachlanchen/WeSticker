WeSticker Privacy

Your account identity, private uploads and creations are stored to operate your workspace. Original photo metadata is removed. Uploaded references are scheduled for deletion after 30 days; generated art stays until you delete it.

The saved local photo draft stays on your device. Uploading references to a connected workspace is a separate action. AI generation sends selected references to the configured provider only after you confirm generation. Chat messages are sent to the configured chat provider when you send them.

Private uploads are not marketplace examples. Public sharing and optional WeChat draft upload require separate consent. The upload assistant does not submit a platform review.

You can report content, export your creations and request account deletion in the web workspace. For help or deletion requests, contact hello@lazying.art.
